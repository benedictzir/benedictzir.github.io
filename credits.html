Project artwork

The artwork and screenshots for Nail Bound, Pacil Explore, Cards, Camera, Action!, and Jumpin'Vim are from their published game pages. They illustrate team projects I contributed to as a developer. I do not claim sole authorship of the game artwork, and these images are not offered as reusable stock assets.

Project images are authentic game artwork and captures of the actual applications, used with the owner's permission. Technology logos are from Devicon, stored locally with their MIT notice. The separate GDScript document icon is from the original Godot Engine repository under its MIT license. The portrait is the owner's supplied photograph; its web version has a very light, non-generative tonal and sharpness correction. The original photograph is retained separately in the owner's archive.

Interface icons

The coffee illustration is from Open Doodles by Pablo Stanley, released under CC0. Its accent is adapted to apricot, with ink blue or pale blue linework for each theme. Path geometry is unchanged. This is a decorative illustration, not a portrait of Benedictus.

The hand-drawn arrow, shooting star, award star, underline, and flower come from dddoodle by Seb / fffuel, used under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Their SVG viewBoxes are trimmed to the drawing bounds; they are displayed with adapted colors, sizing, rotation, and CSS animation. Original pack license notice.

Phosphor Icons, regular weight. MIT license and copyright notice. Icons are served from this website, without a third-party icon CDN.

## Authentic identity marks — 3 November 2026 refinement

The owner rejected emoji achievement illustrations. Main competition identities can use the five exact original marks listed in `data/competitions.json` under `logo`, with explicit rank text kept alongside them. Some identify an organizer or track sponsor rather than the competition itself; `identity` and `logoAlt` record that distinction. These marks remain their respective owners' property. No open-license or new license grant is claimed.

- `icpc-foundation.svg`: original ICPC Foundation mark linked as the logo preview/download by the official [ICPC press page](https://news.icpc.global/press/). Exact source: https://news.icpc.global/icpc_foundation.svg . Used to identify ICPC; it is not a winners' badge or a World Finals participation claim. Original SVG bytes, including its original proportions, are unchanged.
- `gemastik-2026.svg`: genuine XIX/19 horizontal wordmark referenced in the official GEMASTIK site's application bundle. Exact source: https://gemastik.kemdiktisaintek.go.id/assets/images/Gemastik%2019%20-%20horizontal.svg . It may identify XIX/2026 only; do not use it for XVIII/2025. The shared data default instead uses the ministry's genuine generic organizer emblem, documented in ../organizations/SOURCES.md; the wide organizer wordmark is retained as a fallback.
- `gemastik-brand-master.svg`: exact original from https://gemastik.kemdiktisaintek.go.id/assets/images/brand-graphic-master.svg , linked by the official page. This is a tall background graphic, not a verified generic competition logo. It is unselected and should not be used as the achievement identity. Its upstream UTF-16 encoding is preserved.

No standalone GAMESEED wordmark was verified in this bounded pass. Its actual organizer's AGI mark is used instead of reducing the busy official poster to an unreadable thumbnail. No generic or XVIII-specific standalone GEMASTIK logo was verified. Existing exact certificates still establish those editions and results.

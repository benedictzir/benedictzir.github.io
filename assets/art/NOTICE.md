Export comments and whitespace were removed; path geometry is unchanged. These are decorative illustrations, not portraits or original artwork by the portfolio owner. Their original black #000000 is adapted to ink blue #25324A in light mode and pale blue #D4DEEE in dark mode.

The original pink #FF5678 is adapted per illustration:

| Illustration | Light accent | Dark accent |
| ------------ | ------------ | ----------- |
| Coffee       | #E6A476      | #F2B18C     |

Native picture sources follow device preference; the theme toggle selects the matching illustration variants explicitly.
